The Philosophy of Structural Reduction

We believe architecture is not about decoration. It is about the articulation of boundary, the texture of concrete, and the calibration of openings to align with the sun.

01 / Rigor

Every axis is considered. Every alignment serves a purpose. We refine plans to their absolute essentials, eliminating structural noise to emphasize purity of form.

02 / Materiality

Raw concrete, structural steel, untreated timber, and low-iron glass. We select materials that age honestly and respond directly to the northwest climate.
